## What it does

- **One photo** → your character replaces the central performer in a fixed street-freestyle template — shoulder-led movement, a partner with a portable speaker, a watching crowd
- **Three templates**: Fan freestyle (15s, red-lit venue), Performer remix (15s), Character bounce (6s, blue character) — the source video is supplied, no video upload needed
- **720p or 1080p** — 1 credit/sec at 720p, 2 credits/sec at 1080p; shorter output returns unused credits
- No free generation allowance; confirmed failures return credits; completed videos are private MP4s in My videos

## How to use

1. Open https://bulin47ai.site and pick a template (Fan freestyle, Performer remix or Character bounce)
2. Add one clear character photo (JPG/PNG/WebP, up to 10 MB, each side over 340 px) — keep the face, shoulders and outfit visible
3. Sign in, choose 720p or 1080p, review the duration and credit quote, then generate and find your MP4 in My videos
